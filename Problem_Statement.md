# Job Connect – Problem Statement

## Problem Statement

Finding suitable job opportunities and managing applications can be difficult for job seekers. Employers also need an easy way to create job postings, manage applications, and find suitable candidates.

Job Connect is a web-based job portal designed to connect job seekers and employers through a single platform. Job seekers can view available jobs, search for suitable positions, and apply for jobs. Employers can create and manage job postings and view applications from candidates.

The system aims to make the recruitment process simpler, faster, and more organized for both job seekers and employers.
